# Photos

This page lists the free and paid photo sources from the research notes, plus museum and public-domain archives. Each entry gives the licence as read from official licence pages where the fetch succeeded, and the live, redirect or shutdown status checked with `curl -L` on 5 Nov 2026. Several official pages blocked automated access (403/429 bot checks). A blocked or timed-out site is **not** evidence that it shut down, and these cases are flagged. The page ends with guidance on getting art-directed, non-generic imagery. Delivery formats (AVIF, `srcset`) are covered in [optimization.md](optimization.md).

## 1. Key changes 2025–26

- **Reshot** retired in January 2026.
- **Burst** moved to shopify.com/stock-photos.
- **EyeEm** shut down on 16 Jan 2026.
- **Unsplash+** images appear inside normal Unsplash search, under a different licence.
- **Picjumbo and Gratisography now mix in AI-generated images.**
- "Free for commercial use" ≠ CC0. Most custom licences forbid redistribution as stock.

## 5. Licence-safety tiers

These are inferences in the notes.

| Tier | Sources |
|---|---|
| **A: CC0, no strings** | StockSnap, ISO Republic, Nappy, Met / Smithsonian open-access CC0 items |
| **B: custom free licence, no credit, but no resale/redistribution or AI clauses** | Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, Burst, Barnimages, Gratisography, Kaboompics Standard |
| **C: per-file, check every asset** | Wikimedia Commons, Openverse |
| **Avoid for commercial use** | **Kaboompics "Editorial Use Only"** images |

Also from the notes:

- Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, Barnimages and Gratisography all forbid redistribution as stock.
- Unsplash+ explicitly bans AI/ML use.
- **A theme or template that bundles stock images is a redistribution risk, so use placeholders or CC0 in templates.**
- Curated lists from 2023–24 (Muzli, Toptal and others) are likely to contain dead links.

## 6. Quality and aesthetic notes (editorial)

- **Generic-stock risk is highest** on Pixabay, Pexels, Burst and Picjumbo. Picjumbo and Gratisography now mix in AI images. (editorial)
- **More distinctive** (editorial):
  - Kaboompics: consistent, colour-coordinated interiors and lifestyle, with palette metadata.
  - Nappy: representation.
  - Gratisography: quirky and whimsical.
  - Museum open-access collections: art and archival textures, very non-generic.
  - NASA: space imagery.
  - Paid Stocksy and Death to Stock: art-directed, "authentic".

## 7. Getting art-directed, non-generic imagery

These suggestions are inference in the notes, not directly cited.

1. **Moodboard first.** Build moodboards in [Are.na](https://www.are.na/), [Cosmos](https://www.cosmos.so/) or [Savee](https://savee.com/) before searching (see [inspiration-galleries.md](inspiration-galleries.md)).
2. **Search by colour or palette and treat images consistently:** duotone, grain, a single crop ratio, colour-grading LUTs.
3. **Use public-domain archival or museum art and NASA imagery as texture.**
4. **Prefer one photographer's series** (Unsplash and Pexels profiles) over mixed search results.
5. **Commission or shoot product imagery.**
6. **Generate abstract backgrounds** with [Haikei](https://haikei.app/) or [fffuel](https://www.fffuel.co/) instead of using stock photos (see [design-tools.md](design-tools.md)).
