# Mockups

This page lists the device and product mockup sources and generators from the research notes. Each entry has the live status checked with `curl` on 5 Nov 2026 and the page title as served. The notes did not verify licence or pricing pages for these tools, so read each site's terms before using a mockup in commercial marketing. Two notable changes: **Anthony Boyd Graphics is now Minimal Mockups**, and **Screely appears defunct** (its domain shows a hosting placeholder).
