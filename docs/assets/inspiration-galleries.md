# Inspiration Galleries

This page lists the whole-site and UI-pattern inspiration galleries from the research notes. Each entry has its live, redirect or rebrand status checked with `curl` on 5 Nov 2026. The notable changes are:

- **Godly** now redirects to **Recent** (recent.design).
- **Screenlane** redirects to **Page Flows**.
- **Savee** moved to savee.com.
- **Framer Gallery** moved under /community.
- **hyperframe.io** is parked.

The page also covers how to use galleries and moodboards to get a distinctive result rather than a copy of the current trend. Curated lists from 2023–24 (Muzli, Toptal and others) are likely to contain dead links, so prefer this list and re-verify at publish time.

## 7. How to use galleries

The notes give one concrete step: build moodboards in Are.na, Cosmos or Savee **before** searching for stock imagery. They also say that ownership changes (Godly → Recent) mean directories should be re-verified at publish time. Everything else in this section is editorial guidance.

> Editor's note: a workflow that turns galleries into distinctive output.
> 1. **Collect across categories, not just your niche.** Pair one award gallery (Awwwards, The FWA, Recent) with one niche gallery (Brutalist Websites, Hover States, Dark Mode Design). This avoids copying the dominant SaaS template.
> 2. **Use pattern libraries for structure, galleries for character.** Mobbin, Page Flows, Refero, Navbar Gallery, Footer gallery and Supahero answer "how is this usually laid out?". Award and curated galleries answer "how can it feel different?".
> 3. **Moodboard first.** Put 15–30 references in one Are.na, Cosmos or Savee board. Then name the 3–5 recurring traits (type, colour, motion, imagery treatment) before you open a design tool.
> 4. **Borrow the system, not the screen.** Extract principles (scale contrast, grid, motion timing) and rebuild them with your own assets: [fonts.md](fonts.md), [design-tools.md](design-tools.md), [photos.md](photos.md).
> 5. **Check motion references at the source.** GSAP Showcase and Codrops show how effects are built (see [../animations/](../animations/README.md)).
