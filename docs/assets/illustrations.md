# Illustrations

This page lists the illustration libraries from the research notes, with licences checked on official licence pages as of 5 Nov 2026. It also explains the "Corporate Memphis" look and how to avoid it. Many free illustration sets have custom licences with restrictions that are easy to miss:

- **unDraw and DrawKit** forbid AI/ML training.
- **Absurd Design's free licence** is non-commercial only.
- **Storyset** requires attribution.
- Several older sources (Stubborn, Popsy, Delesign) are down or unverifiable.

3D illustration renders are on [3d-and-animated-icons.md](3d-and-animated-icons.md).

## 3. Corporate Memphis and how to avoid it

**What it is.** Corporate Memphis ("Alegria") is a flat style with "long, elastic arms, small heads", odd skin colours (purple or blue) and stock scenes such as high-fiving and watering plants.

- Buck created it for Facebook in 2017. Slack, Google and others copied it.
- Stock sites spread it, **including unDraw**.
- *Creative Bloq* said that by 2023 its popularity had fallen because of "uninspired design" and oversaturation.
- *Fast Company* (2022) reported a return toward 3D and skeuomorphism.

Source: [Wikipedia: Corporate Memphis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporate_Memphis).

**Sources by risk of looking generic** (from the style notes above):

| Risk | Sources |
|---|---|
| High risk of the generic look | unDraw, Humaaans, Storyset (Freepik stock), IRA Design (dated gradient flat) |
| Medium (widely used) | Open Peeps |
| More distinctive options | Blush (pick a specific, less common artist), Absurd Design (paid membership for commercial use), Streamline premium sets |

> Editor's note: other routes to non-generic visuals in this doc set include the following.
> - 3D renders (see [3d-and-animated-icons.md](3d-and-animated-icons.md)), in line with the reported shift toward 3D and skeuomorphism.
> - Generated SVG shapes and textures from Haikei, fffuel, Blobmaker and Hero Patterns (see [design-tools.md](design-tools.md)).
> - Public-domain museum art and archival imagery (see [photos.md](photos.md)).
> - Commissioning a custom illustrator.
>
> The notes recommend each of these routes for imagery in general. None is specific to illustration.

## 4. Licence-safety tiers and AI-training bans

These are inferences in the notes.

| Tier | Libraries |
|---|---|
| **CC0 / permissive, no credit** | Open Peeps, Humaaans, Open Doodles (CC0). IRA Design (MIT) |
| **Free, no credit, custom licence with restrictions** | unDraw, DrawKit, Blush, ManyPixels. Lukasz Adam has an ambiguous licence statement |
| **Credit required** | Storyset (free), IconScout (free) |
| **Avoid for commercial work** | **Absurd Design free licence** (non-commercial only) |

**AI workflows.** unDraw, DrawKit and Absurd explicitly ban AI training. Using their assets on a site is fine, but feeding them into a model is not.
